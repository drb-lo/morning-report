OpenAI floats a surprisingly reasonable frontier-AI governance blueprint

Zvi Mowshowitz · Don't Worry About the Vase · NEW ~1d

Landing right after Trump's new AI-testing executive order, OpenAI's “Democratic Governance of Frontier AI” would empower CAISI — not the NSA — to run mandatory, time-boxed evaluations of the most capable models on the SB 53 model, paired with transparency, auditing, incident reporting and whistleblower protections. The trade is federal preemption of state frontier-safety laws. Zvi calls it a serious starting point that beats expectations, but is wary the preemption could prove toothless, selectively enforced, or foreclose stronger state action.
