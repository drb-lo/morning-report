America doesn’t need a single national story

Writing in the libertarian magazine Reason ahead of next month’s 250th anniversary, Matt Welch argues that the longing for one unifying patriotic narrative is misplaced — the 1976 bicentennial was itself fractious and contested, not a moment of shared consensus.
