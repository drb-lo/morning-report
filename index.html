05Medicine Clinical

FDA clears the first oral GLP-1 pill for weight management

The FDA approved oral semaglutide for chronic weight management, the first GLP-1 pill cleared for obesity, on the strength of the Phase 3 OASIS 4 trial — which showed 16.6% mean weight loss at 64 weeks with full adherence versus 2.7% for placebo, with about a third of adherent patients losing at least 20%. A pill option could widen access and adherence beyond injectables, though dosing-window and cost questions remain.
