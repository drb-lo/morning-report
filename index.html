07BlindsideSteelman

The hidden human cost of blocking GMO “Golden Rice”

Reason science correspondent Ronald Bailey spotlights a new estimate by researcher Abi Olvera that the decades-long activist campaign against Golden Rice — a grain genetically engineered to supply the vitamin A precursor beta-carotene — has, through delay, contributed to roughly 106,000 child deaths and left 210,000–425,000 children blind across 11 rice-dependent countries, since vitamin A deficiency is the world's leading preventable cause of childhood blindness. He revisits a 2016 letter from 100 Nobel laureates urging Greenpeace to drop its opposition, and argues that precaution against new technology carries its own measurable body count. It lands with libertarian and pro-biotech readers because it turns a cause associated with environmental caution into a humanitarian indictment — pressing the left's precautionary, anti-GMO instincts against the yardstick of lives lost.
